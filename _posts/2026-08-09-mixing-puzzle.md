Mixing puzzles appear when mixing recipes or completing the Rite of Clearing on adventures.

![Mixing Battle](/assets/img/pw_battle.jpg)

### Gameplay:
- Clear out all of the ingredients by combining pieces to form lines.
- Forming lines with 9+ pieces create arrow pieces. These arrow pieces allow you to deal damage using element pieces.
    - Making a line of 18+ pieces will create a double arrow which damages the selected target and the target beside them.
- Clearing out the bosses in the middle (indicated by being bigger in size) will also eliminate the minion pieces when defeated. 

### Mixing Pieces:
- **Energy**: Yellow pieces that deal damage to the selected enemy
- **Element**: Star/moon/sun pieces that provide you shield or can damage enemies when used with an arrow piece
    - Star deals extra damage to sun, sun extra damage to moon, moon extra damage to star
    - If you have a shield, using element pieces with an arrow will use your shield to deal extra damage
- **Hourglasses**: Delays the selected enemy from attacking you
- **Stamina**: Pink pieces that recover your health

### Wands and Charms:
- You can craft wands or charms in the Wandery to aid you in mixes!
- After a certain number of turns you can use wands to deal damage, delay attacks, or heal yourself
- Charms boost the effectiveness of certain mixing pieces (ex. Extra damage for energy pieces)

### Multiplayer Mixes:
- When mixing with other players a piece may appear with their avatar's face
- Players deal combined damage to big bosses, so make sure to get some friends for tough mixes
- You can use this piece in a variety of different ways:
    - Faces are similar to arrow pieces, allowing you to use them with any mixing piece 
    - Use their face with stamina pieces to heal the other player
    - Use their face with energy or element pieces to deal damage

> If you are getting knocked out frequently try experimenting with different wands and charms!
