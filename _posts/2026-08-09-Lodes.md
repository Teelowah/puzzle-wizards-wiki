Lodes are resources that you can mine from boulder puzzles. 

![Lodes](assets/img/lodes.png) {: .right}
_Lodes_

The following lodes are used to mix building materials and recipes:
- Clay
- Stone
- Iron

The following rare lodes are found on adventures or by doing well in boulder puzzles.
- Azurite
- Carnelian
- Celestine
- Cinnabar
- Jade
- Olivine
- Ruby
- Sapphire

> If you are searching for specific rare lodes they can be found in the dungeons in your adventures.
