Lodes are resources that you can mine from boulder puzzles. 

![Lodes]({{ '/assets/img/lodes.png' | relative_url }})
_Lodes_

The following lodes are used to mix building materials and recipes:
- Clay
- Stone
- Iron

You can find minerals from lode puzzles on adventures in the dungeons or by doing well in boulder puzzles.
- Azurite
- Carnelian
- Celestine
- Cinnabar
- Jade
- Olivine
- Ruby
- Sapphire

In the first attempt at lode puzzles, strong boulders will yield a mineral one rarity higher than your current harvest.
- In sun woods: Stone -> Cinnabar -> Carnelian -> Ruby
- In moon woods: Clay -> Azurite -> Celestine -> Sapphire
- In star woods: Iron -> Olivine -> Jade -> Emerald

> If you are looking for a specific mineral you can keep starting new adventures until one with a dungeon appears
