You will start your puzzle wizards journey in the home village!

## Buildings
Buildings provide a various different types of functions for the main village.

- **Apothecary**: Where you can make tonics, dyes, and more.
- **Bakery**: Make food to grow your fams
- **Cloudkeep Cottage**: A castle building similar to your house
- **Hattery**: Where you can make hats for your character
- **Moon Hut**: Where you can make moon soil and convert crops
- **Player's House**: Your home
- **Sun Hut**: Where you can make sun soil and convert crops
- **Star Hut**: Where you can make star soil and convert crops
- **Tailory**: Where you can make shirts for your character
- **Wandery**: Where you can make wands and charms
- **Workshop**: Where you can craft decor items and building materials

## Village Name
You can rename your village by clicking on the book beside the Heartwood Tree, and clicking the edit button at the bottom of the pop up window.

## Building Upgrades
Buildings can be upgraded up to level 3. 

To upgrade them, click the top of the building where the standout feature is (ex. Workshop windmill, Apothecary Potion). This will bring up the list of items that must be mixed/harvested as well as how many sparks you need to spend unlocking recipes.

Once all requirements have been fulfilled, the building will be available to upgrade the following day. On upgrade day you can add building materials to upgrade the building.

> To move your buildings in the future will cost 5 gold coins. Make sure you use your sparks to expand your village before you place them!
