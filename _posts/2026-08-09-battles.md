Battles occur when facing enemies on adventures

### Gameplay:
- Defeat all enemies by creating and matching smiley face pieces
- Slide pieces in their rows to form matches
- Matching 4 pieces will result in smiley face pieces
    - Matching smiley face pieces will deal damage to your selected enemy
- Select which enemy to target by tapping on them

### Enemy Types:
- **Star Minions**: The minions turn pieces into bombs that black out rows near it.
> Clear out bombs by creating matches with those pieces as soon as possible
{: .prompt-tip }
- **Frozen Ghosts**: Create frozen pieces that you can clear by making matches near them
- **Tree Minions**: Create vined pieces that you have to clear
- **Fire Minions**: Create fires that char pieces in the columns in which they appear 
- **Big Bosses**: Similar to star minions but spawn multiple bombs at once

### Battle Wands and Charms
There are several wands you can use in battle with abilities such as dealing damage or clearing obstacles.

Wands:
- Deicer Wand: Clears *frozen* pieces
- Scrubber Wand: Reduces *charred* pieces
- Shazap Wand: Deals damage to enemies (Damage increases by 10 per wand level)
- Weed Whacker Wand: Clears *vined* pieces

Charms:
- Iron Fists Charm: Adds damage to energy piece attacks
- Kindling Charm: Adds *burn* pieces that damage and burn the target enemy
- Pump it up Charm: Boosts energy pieces by one level

### Multiplayer Battles:
You can battle enemies with friends. Make sure you both select the same enemy to attack to defeat them quickly!
