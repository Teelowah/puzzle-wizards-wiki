Guilds are shared villages where you can go on select adventures and participate in community events!

## Guild Buildings
The main buildings in the guild are:
- **Moon Tower**: Go on adventures in the Moon Woods
- **Sun Tower**: Go on adventures in the Sun Woods
- **Star Tower**: Go on adventures in the Star Woods
- **Tavern**: Brew recipes to make items for your guild

### Guild Towers
- To build the towers you can go to the guild mixing pot

- Levelling up towers, by going on adventures, will provide you with access to select adventures in the inner, middle, or outer woods areas (easy access to farm rare minerals)

- At each tower you can buy adventure maps with dazzle to unlock wands, flowers, fam eggs, and statues.

### Guild Resources
- **Bonds**: Bonds are used to craft guild items, advance in events, and more. You can earn bonds by completing sections of adventures with 2 or more players.
- **Event Dust**: Event dust is earned by completing adventures during events such as monument rallies. Dust can be used to craft event items during event periods.
